# Landmarks and heading structure

**Scoring area:** Accessibility (93)

## Why this earns a high score
Semantic HTML helps assistive technology navigate.

## Evidence in your project
- Uses `<aside>`, `<header>`, `<main>`, `<section>` and `<footer>`.
- One `<h1>`, followed by `<h2>` and `<h3>` in order.
- Real `<button>`, `<select>`, `<label>` and `<input>` elements, not clickable divs.

## Takeaway
Page structure is meaningful and keyboard-friendly by default.
