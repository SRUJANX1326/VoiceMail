# Trust and user control

**Scoring area:** Problem Statement Alignment (9)

## Why this earns a high score
A practical assistant must be safe to rely on.

## Evidence in your project
- Explicit address confirmation, final send confirmation, correction at any time, cancel phrases, and automatic cancel after repeated silence ("Nothing was sent").
- Natural-language yes/no recognition ("yep", "that would work", "not now").

## Takeaway
Users stay in control of every irreversible step.
