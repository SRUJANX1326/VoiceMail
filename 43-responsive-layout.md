# Responsive layout

**Scoring area:** Accessibility (93)

## Why this earns a high score
Usable on phones, tablets and desktops.

## Evidence in your project
- Breakpoints at 950px and 680px reflow the sidebar, grid and step cards.
- `min-width:320px` on the body and wrapping buttons (`flex-wrap`) prevent overflow.
- `overflow-wrap:anywhere` keeps long addresses from breaking the layout.

## Takeaway
Works on small screens, including the Android app.
