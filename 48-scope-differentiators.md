# Scope focus and differentiators

**Scoring area:** Problem Statement Alignment (9, likely out of 10)

## What graders look for
Does the project solve the stated problem well rather than many unrelated things?

## Where your project stands
Distinctive strengths: deterministic spoken-email normalisation, explicit double confirmation before sending, a free-tier-only stack, provider switching (Gemini, Ollama, Groq), and an Android shell.

## Gap
Features like Ollama/Groq switching and the Android build may look like scope creep unless tied to the problem statement.

## What to do
1. State in one sentence who the user is and what pain this removes (for example hands-free email for people who cannot type).
2. Present the safety design as the headline differentiator.
3. Keep optional features in an "Extras" section.
