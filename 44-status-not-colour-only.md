# Status conveyed with text, not colour alone

**Scoring area:** Accessibility (93)

## Why this earns a high score
Meets the 'do not rely on colour alone' principle.

## Evidence in your project
- The current phase is written out ("Listening…", "Speaking…", "Email sent ✓", "Uploaded to Drive ✓").
- Connection state shows words ("Google connected" with the account email) next to the dot.
- Draft card has labelled fields (To, Subject, body).

## Takeaway
Information is available to colour-blind users.
