# Good contrast and a voice-first design

**Scoring area:** Accessibility (93)

## Why this earns a high score
The design itself widens access.

## Evidence in your project
- Main body text is near-white on near-black, and I measured muted text `#858b92` on its panel at 5.62:1 and the orange accent at 6.03:1, both above the WCAG AA 4.5:1 threshold.
- The product is hands-free by design: wake word, spoken confirmations, spoken reading of drafts. That directly helps users who cannot type or see a screen easily.

## Takeaway
Accessibility is part of the product's purpose, not an add-on.
