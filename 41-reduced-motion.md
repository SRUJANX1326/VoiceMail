# Respects reduced-motion preference

**Scoring area:** Accessibility (93)

## Why this earns a high score
Prevents discomfort for motion-sensitive users.

## Evidence in your project
- `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)` sets animation durations to about zero and disables transitions and smooth scrolling.
- Applied globally and again for the live line and transcript.

## Takeaway
A WCAG-aligned motion safeguard.
