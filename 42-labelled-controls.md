# Labelled form controls

**Scoring area:** Accessibility (93)

## Why this earns a high score
Every input has a name.

## Evidence in your project
- The interrupt toggle and the voice selector are wrapped in `<label>` elements with descriptive text.
- Buttons carry visible text ("Start listening", "Stop listening", "Connect Google", "Interrupt voice").

## Takeaway
Controls are understandable to everyone.
