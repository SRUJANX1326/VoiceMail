# Voice interface plus AI generation

**Scoring area:** Problem Statement Alignment (9)

## Why this earns a high score
Uses voice and AI in a way that genuinely fits the task.

## Evidence in your project
- Wake phrase "Hey my assistant", hands-free conversation and barge-in (interrupt by speaking).
- The AI writes an original email of about 200 words from spoken instructions, with no templates.
- If the user describes everything in one sentence, the details are reused instead of asked again.

## Takeaway
Natural interaction rather than a form with a microphone.
